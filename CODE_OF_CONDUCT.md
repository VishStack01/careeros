# Code of conduct

Propelo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome people who are new to open source or still looking for their first job, and keep feedback about the work, not the person. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information are not accepted anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions or messages.

Many people here are in the middle of a job search, which is stressful. Please don't post anyone's resume, contact details or application history, including your own, in public threads.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to the maintainer privately through GitHub (the contact on [@VishStack01](https://github.com/VishStack01)'s profile). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, or block people who break this code, from the project.
