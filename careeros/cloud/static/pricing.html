Pricing

Finding and checking roles is free for everyone. Pro is for when you want them every hour, with AI that writes from your real experience.

Can I use my own AI key?

Yes, on any plan. Save an Anthropic, OpenAI-compatible or Gemini key in Settings and AI actions run on it without counting toward your allowance. It's stored encrypted.

How do I pay?

UPI, cards or netbanking through Razorpay. We never see your card or bank details. Pro renews monthly until you cancel.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in Settings. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for, then move to Free with all your data. See refunds.

Does Propelo apply on LinkedIn or Naukri?

No. It reads company career pages and applies from your own computer only where it can answer every question truthfully. Roles listed only on the big boards are marked "Apply yourself".
