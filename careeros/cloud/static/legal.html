Privacy notice

What we store

  • Your account: name, email, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), sign-in sessions and security events such as sign-ins and password changes.
  • Your job search: the filters you set, the roles matched to you and what you did with them, notes, and the history of each application.
  • Your application details: what you enter on the setup page (contact details, links, experience, notice period, pay expectations, education, work authorization) and the resume files you upload.
  • Your plan: which plan you're on, your subscription's status and renewal date, and the payment provider's subscription reference. We never see or store card, UPI or bank details; Razorpay handles payment and holds them under its own privacy policy.
  • AI usage: how many AI actions you used each month. If you save your own model key, it's encrypted, and only its last four characters are ever shown.

We never ask for or store passwords to job sites, one-time codes, Aadhaar, PAN, passport or bank details, or your date of birth.

How it's used

Only to run your job search: matching roles to your filters, showing your pipeline, preparing applications, and giving your own apply agent (on your computer, with a key you create) the details it puts into application forms. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI actions

When you use an AI action (reading a posting, scoring fit, tailoring a resume or cover letter), the text needed for that action is sent to the model provider: the service's provider on your plan's allowance, or the provider of your own key. Providers process it to answer the request under their API terms. We don't use your data to train models.

Where applications go

Applications are submitted from your computer's browser, by an agent you set up, to the employer's own application page. The employer then holds what you sent under their own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Export: download everything we hold about you from Settings, Account.
  • Delete: deleting your account removes your account, documents, files, keys and sessions right away. Encrypted backups roll over within 30 days.
  • Correct: edit your details at any time.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Sessions are HttpOnly cookies, sign-in attempts are rate limited, and every request for your data is checked against your account. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you think your account was misused.

Terms of use

  • CareerOS is provided as is. Roles come from public career pages and can be wrong, closed or out of date. Check a posting before relying on it. CareerOS doesn't promise interviews or offers.
  • You're responsible for what's submitted in your name. Only enter true information, and only queue or allow applications you actually want to send.
  • Don't use CareerOS to break any website's terms, to apply through sites that forbid automation, or to send spam.
  • Keep your computer keys and model keys secret. Remove a key in Settings if it may have leaked.
  • Plans. The Free plan costs nothing. Pro is billed monthly in Indian rupees, in advance, through Razorpay, and renews automatically until you cancel. Prices include any taxes shown at checkout. If a renewal payment fails, Razorpay retries it for a few days; if it still fails, your account moves to Free and keeps all your data.
  • AI allowance. Each plan includes a number of AI actions per calendar month on the service's model. Unused actions don't carry over. AI actions on your own key don't count toward it.
  • We may suspend accounts that abuse the service, and we may change these terms or prices; we'll tell you by email at least 14 days before a material change or a price rise takes effect.

Refunds and cancellation

Cancel any time in Settings, Plan. Cancelling stops future charges; you keep Pro until the end of the month you've paid for, then your account moves to Free with all your data.

Refunds. If you were charged by mistake (a duplicate charge, or a renewal after you cancelled), write to us within 7 days and we refund it in full to the original payment method. If Pro didn't work for you in your first 7 days, ask and we'll refund that first payment. Refunds reach you in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.

Delivery. CareerOS is an online service; nothing is shipped. Pro features switch on as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute.
