Privacy notice

What we store

  • Your account: name, email, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), sign-in sessions and security events such as sign-ins and password changes.
  • Your job search: the filters you set, the roles matched to you and what you did with them, notes, and the history of each application.
  • Your application details: what you enter on the setup page (contact details, links, experience, notice period, pay expectations, education, work authorization) and the resume files you upload.

We never ask for or store passwords to job sites, one-time codes, Aadhaar, PAN, passport or bank details, or your date of birth.

How it's used

Only to run your job search: matching roles to your filters, showing your pipeline, and giving your own apply agent (on your computer, with a key you create) the details it puts into application forms you've chosen or allowed. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or show it to other users. Job listings are shared between all users; nothing about you is.

Where applications go

Applications are submitted from your computer's browser, by an agent you set up, to the employer's own application page. The employer then holds what you sent under their own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Export: download everything we hold about you from Brain & setup → Account.
  • Delete: deleting your account removes your account, documents, files, keys and sessions right away. Encrypted backups roll over within 30 days.
  • Correct: edit your details on the setup page at any time.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Sessions are HttpOnly cookies, and sign-in attempts are rate limited. Every request for your data is checked against your account. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you notice anything wrong.

Terms of use

  • CareerOS is in beta and provided as is. Roles come from public career pages and can be wrong, closed or out of date. Check a posting before relying on it.
  • You're responsible for what's submitted in your name. Only enter true information, and only queue or allow applications you actually want to send.
  • Don't use CareerOS to break any website's terms, to apply through sites that forbid automation, or to send spam.
  • Keep your computer keys secret. Anyone with one can read your pipeline and report applications for you. Remove a key from the setup page if it may have leaked.
  • We may suspend accounts that abuse the service, and we may change these terms; we'll tell you by email before material changes take effect.

Contact

Questions, data requests or security reports: write to the operator of this CareerOS service at the address on the sign-up email you received.
