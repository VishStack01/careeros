What it does

It searches company job boards every hour, keeps the roles that fit your filters and shows why each one passed. It scores your fit with evidence, tailors your resume line by line, and tracks every reply in one place.

Why it's different

Nothing is invented. Every resume line comes from your profile, every role links to the company's own posting, and nothing is sent without you. It never applies on LinkedIn or Naukri for you, so your accounts stay safe.
