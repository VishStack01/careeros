# How Propelo compares

Four products shaped the 2.0 feature set: Simplify, Teal, Jobright and Salvo. This page lists what each is known for, what Propelo built in response, and where Propelo deliberately does something different. Their features and prices change often; this reflects their public pages and reviews in November 2026. Nothing here copies their code, design or text.

## At a glance

| | Simplify | Teal | Jobright | Salvo | Propelo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where roles come from | Job boards and its own list | Any board you save from | Its own index plus boards | A live index of employer ATS pages | ~1,400 company career pages, scanned hourly, matched every 15 minutes on Ultra |
| Why a role was kept | Match score | Keyword match | Match score | Fit summary | A quote from the posting for every check, and every skip logged with its reason |
| Resume tailoring | AI rewrite | AI writing tools | Tailored resume | Tailored per role | Only facts from your profile; a claims checker flags anything it can't trace |
| Applying | Autofill extension; paid autopilot | Manual | Autofill; AI agent | Auto-apply on employer ATS | Autofill extension, and auto-apply from your own computer above a 4.0 fit line |
| Recruiter emails | No | Templates | Referral intros | Sent automatically | Drafted, or sent from your own Gmail when you turn it on, with limits and an opt-out |
| Copilot | No | No | Yes (Orion) | No | Ask Propelo, grounded on your pipeline |
| Scam check | No | No | Yes | Posting check | Rule-based, quoting the words that triggered it; unsafe postings are never auto-applied |
| India pricing | USD only | USD only | USD only | USD only | ₹0 / ₹499 / ₹1,199, with UPI |
| Open source, self-hostable | No | No | No | No | Yes, MIT |

Approximate paid prices seen: Teal about $29 for 30 days, Jobright Turbo $39.99 a month, Salvo Turbo $49 a month. Propelo: $22.50 (Pro) and $48.50 (Ultra) outside India.

## What Propelo added for 2.0, and where the idea came from

| Feature | Seen at | Propelo's version |
|---|---|---|
| Contacts | Teal | A Contacts page with linked roles, last contact and follow-up dates, filled from your roles' outreach in one click |
| Save from any job site | Teal, Simplify | The extension's **Save to Propelo** reads the page's JobPosting data on company sites. On LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed and Wellfound it reads nothing from the page: only the link and tab title |
| Copilot | Jobright | **Ask Propelo**, answering from your profile, pipeline and filters, with links into the app; capped by plan |
| Application receipts | Salvo | Every auto-applied role keeps the confirmation text and the fields filled (never sensitive ones), printable |
| Recruiter and referral emails | Salvo, Jobright | Drafted by default. Opt-in sending from your Gmail: approved template, public-source addresses only, 10 or 25 a day, follow-ups on day 7 and 14, stops on reply, global opt-out |
| High volume | Salvo | Ultra allows up to 1,000 applications a month, each still above the apply line and through the scam check |
| Interview prep page | Salvo | A one-page prep sheet per role: likely questions mapped to your own stories, honest answers for gaps, questions to ask |
| Mock interviews | Salvo, Jobright | Already in Propelo; now counted per plan |
| LinkedIn profile review | Jobright and others | You paste your profile text; Propelo never opens LinkedIn |
| Scam detection | Jobright | Shared rules on the server and in the app, with the quote that triggered each warning |

## Where Propelo is different on purpose

- **Every claim is defensible.** Tailoring never adds a skill, number or employer that isn't in your profile, and screening answers it can't back up are left for you.
- **No automation on sites that forbid it.** No LinkedIn messages, Easy Apply, Naukri or Indeed automation, ever.
- **Quality over volume.** High-volume plans still respect the 4.0 apply line, one application per company per six months, and the scam check.
- **Emails are yours.** Automatic emails go from your own mailbox, only to public addresses, with an opt-out that every Propelo user honours.
- **Your data can leave.** Export everything, delete everything, or run the whole thing yourself.
