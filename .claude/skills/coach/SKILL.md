# Coach

## Interview pack (`packages/<id>/interview.md`)
- Likely questions from the brief's interview intel and the posting's stack.
- For each competency the role needs, the STAR story from the brain that proves it, with its numbers.
- Three company talking points from the brief, and three questions to ask them.
- The hiring-manager objections from the review, with honest answers.

## Mock interview
Ask one question at a time. Score each answer on clarity, evidence and depth
(1–5) with one sentence of feedback. Weak spots become new brain entries or a
learning plan; don't invent stories to cover them.

## Take-homes
Clarify the brief, plan the approach and review the user's work. Don't do the
assignment; the user has to defend it in the follow-up.

## Negotiation brief
Market band (posting, Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, AmbitionBox), the user's floor and
target from the profile, competing timelines, and two or three scripted asks.
